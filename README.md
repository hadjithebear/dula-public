# Dula — public research demonstration

**Dula is an independent economic research and development company.** It studies how regional
economies work, in order to identify productive opportunities, develop enterprises, and
coordinate the capital, infrastructure and institutions those enterprises need in order to be
viable.

This repository holds the **public build of Dula's research application**. It is a static site:
the economic model's published artefacts, and the application that reads them.

## What this is, and what it is not

This is a **research demonstration**, not a finished institutional intelligence product and not
an investment offering.

* The economic models are still being developed and validated.
* The opportunity investigations are at the stage of asking whether something is worth looking
  at. None of them is an investable opportunity, and the application says so wherever one
  appears.
* Dula has produced no investment return and holds no track record.
* Two propositions — that understanding economic relationships improves investment selection,
  and that changing those relationships can make a productive investment feasible — are stated
  in the application as propositions under investigation. Neither is a demonstrated performance
  claim.

## What is in here

A regional economic model covering counties in New Mexico: productive systems, the evidence
behind them and its limits, cross-sector dependencies, opportunity discovery, and commercial and
development feasibility. Every figure carries the programme, year, geography and population it
measures, and the application refuses to present a modelled estimate as an observation.
